# KidLearn Visual Style Lock V1.0
Date: 2026-10-27. Approved direction by project owner.

## Locked visual references
- Boy: approved Math learning-card illustration. Short brown hair, large black eyes, rosy cheeks, lively smile, blue-white school outfit and bear motif. Preserve facial identity in all poses.
- Girl: approved Vietnamese learning-card illustration. Brown bob/shoulder hair with fringe and pink ribbons, large black eyes, rosy cheeks, pink/white/blue outfit. Preserve facial identity.
- Robot: **Variant B**, softened blue hero with moderate gloss and ink outlines; preserve blue/cyan armor, large blue eyes, open-book crest, pencil/ruler accessories. Robot remains distinct mascot.

## Art direction
Colorful children's storybook chibi with rounded shapes, clear soft outlines, gentle volume and pastel panels. Do not turn characters into flat minimal SVG or photorealistic 3D. Do not make the robot more glossy than the already accepted variant B. Exact RGB tokens are not locked until visually sampled/approved; maintain the approved reference cards.

## Output and quality
- Single figure: real RGBA transparency (not fake checkerboard, not solid white), no labels, no frame or speech bubble. Master PNG typically 1024×1024 or 768×1024; optimized WebP alpha for app.
- Keep aspect ratio and safe margins ~6-10%; no cropped hair, ribbons, limbs, books. QA on white, cream, sky blue and navy.
- Scenes: separate background and foreground where possible; backgrounds may be opaque.
- The approved cards are references for ART and LAYOUT, NOT authoritative math/text. All questions and answers should be separate validated data.

## Clothing
Everyday style is locked by reference. **School uniform remains optional and not yet approved**: trial white shirt/blue trims/navy bottoms, same faces and hair, in neutral and hand-raise poses.

## Versioning
New or materially revised identity requires owner approval and version increment. Each production asset must have an ID, reference, creator/prompt, right-to-use review, pose/outfit, pixels, alpha QA and approval status.
