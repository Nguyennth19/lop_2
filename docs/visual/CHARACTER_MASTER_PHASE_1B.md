## Existing proof
The separately delivered contact sheets were cropped from the two owner-approved learning cards (five visible poses each). **These are reference crops, not transparent production assets**, and some include pieces of the composite card. Uniform variants are not yet generated.

## Acceptance gates
1. Two isolated master neutral figures approved.
2. Eight everyday poses visually consistent across expression/eyes/hair/clothes.
3. Four optional uniform variants approved separately.
4. Real alpha/clean edges tested on four contrasting colors; printable and usable at 96px in app.
5. Hands, footwear, ribbons, books and anatomy clean; metadata/rights audit.
6. Compare in actual KidLearn homepage and one Math + one Vietnamese electronic worksheet.
Do not commit sample cards as application UI. Educational text/math remains structured data from authoritative SGK/VBT/reference source hierarchy.
